# 🔱 Gold Vault v1.0

![Gold Vault Pro]

**Gold Vault Pro** is a high-performance, private, and secure web application designed for gold investors. Track your gold portfolio (999 & 916), automate Zakat calculations, and monitor live market trends in one beautiful dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Live Demo
**[Click here to open Gold Vault Pro](https://naqiex.github.io/gold-vault/)**

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 📈 **Live Price Syncing:** Real-time 999 and 916 gold rates (MYR/USD) powered by GoldPrice.org API.
* 🕋 **Automated Zakat Tracker:** Automatically calculates your Zakat-payable amount once your 999-equivalent weight crosses the *Nisab* (85g).
* 📊 **Smart Portfolio:** Track True Profit/Loss by accounting for "Spread" (Sell-back prices) and "Workmanship Fees."
* 📱 **PWA Ready:** Install it on your iOS or Android device as a native app—no App Store required.
* 🔒 **Privacy First:** Your data is stored locally in your browser. No account required. No data ever leaves your device.
* 📄 **PDF Reports:** Generate professional inventory reports for your records or tax purposes.
* 📉 **Interactive Charts:** Integrated TradingView charts for technical market analysis.

---

## 🛠️ How to Use

1.  **Add Entry:** Click "New Entry" to add your gold bars, coins, or jewelry.
2.  **Toggle Currency:** Switch between RM (MYR) and $ (USD) in the settings.
3.  **Sync Rates:** Click the refresh icon to pull the latest global gold prices.
4.  **Set Targets:** Update your "Target Price" to see how close you are to your investment goals.
5.  **Backup/Restore:** Use the "Copy Code" feature in settings to backup your data or move it to another device.
